How to read

  • Rows are lines, cool to hot. Columns are instrument cases (Layout).
  • Flux is normalised to the continuum; v is the velocity relative to the galaxy's systemic velocity.
  • Stepped line: the degraded spectrum, a Gaussian line-spread function of FWHM c/R with 5 pixels and the given S/N per resolution element. Shading: the noiseless model. Intrinsic (dashed): the noiseless spectrum before the line-spread function.
  • Fitted components: the fitted model in ink, each component in the line colour and a tick at its centre. A hollow tick marks a component on a bound, cut by the time cap, or unconstrained (log N error above 1 dex). An arrow marks a saturated component: its log N is only a lower limit, read as “log N > 14.5 sat”. The readout gives the fitted values, their errors and flags.
  • Drag or use the arrow keys to pan; ctrl or cmd with the wheel, or + and −, zoom; Home resets.
  • 3D: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a sightline to open its spectra.

Data and methods

Sightlines through the circumgalactic medium of one TNG50-1 galaxy (subhalo 488530 at z = 0), labelled by orientation, impact parameter b and azimuth from the projected major axis.

Optical depths from Trident on a 0.25 km/s grid, stored at 1 km/s. Velocities are relative to the galaxy's systemic velocity.

H I: TNG neutral fraction (self-shielding included); metal ions: Trident HM2012 table, solar ratios scaled by metallicity.

Atomic data follow Trident 1.4.2 (O VI 1031.912 Å and C IV 1548.187 Å, 3 to 4 km/s from Morton 2003); O VII from Verner et al. 1996. Damped H I wings use Trident's Γ = 4.69 × 10⁸ s⁻¹ against the standard damping constant 6.265 × 10⁸ (Morton 2003), so damped log N fitted here sits about 0.13 dex above VPFIT-style fits of the same wings. Saturated components are lower limits.

The degradation and the Voigt fits run in your browser: Levenberg–Marquardt on (log N, b, v) through the line-spread function, seeded from the intrinsic component catalogue and pruned by ΔBIC. Noise matches the publication figure only statistically.

3D: every gas cell and star particle deposited into nested cubes (±400, ±100 and ±25 kpc), fetched from a public Hugging Face dataset. 3D velocities are mass-weighted means per voxel in the galaxy's rest frame (no Hubble flow added; vx, vy and vz each clipped at the top of the 3D velocity window). vlos and σlos follow the camera ray every frame, and positive vlos recedes from you, as in the spectra; σlos is the spread between voxels, without thermal or within-voxel motion. Find searches the fitted components of every sightline. Ask is an optional chat that drives the page through the same tools; its local model downloads about 1 GB, and the Claude backend sends your messages through the sightline server.
