How to read

  • Rows are lines, cool to hot. Columns are instrument cases (Layout).
  • Flux is normalised to the continuum; v is the velocity relative to the galaxy's systemic velocity.
  • Stepped line: the degraded spectrum, a Gaussian line-spread function of FWHM c/R with 5 pixels and the given S/N per resolution element. Shading: the noiseless model. Intrinsic (dashed): the noiseless spectrum before the line-spread function.
  • Fitted components: the fitted model in ink, each component in the line colour and a tick at its centre. A hollow tick marks a component on a bound, cut by the time cap, or unconstrained (log N error above 1 dex); the readout gives the fitted values with their errors.
  • Drag or use the arrow keys to pan; ctrl or cmd with the wheel, or + and −, zoom; Home resets.
  • 3D: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a sightline to open its spectra.

Data and methods

Sightlines through the circumgalactic medium of one TNG50-1 galaxy (subhalo 488530 at z = 0), labelled by orientation, impact parameter b and azimuth from the projected major axis.

Optical depths from Trident on a 0.25 km/s grid, stored at 1 km/s. Velocities are relative to the galaxy's systemic velocity.

The degradation and the Voigt fits run in your browser: Levenberg–Marquardt on (log N, b, v) through the line-spread function, seeded from the intrinsic component catalogue and pruned by ΔBIC. Noise matches the publication figure only statistically.

3D: every gas cell and star particle deposited into nested cubes (±400, ±100 and ±25 kpc), fetched from a public Hugging Face dataset. Find searches the fitted components of every sightline. Ask is an optional chat that drives the page through the same tools; its local model downloads about 1 GB, and the Claude backend sends your messages through the sightline server.
