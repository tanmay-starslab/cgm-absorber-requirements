The page's optional music (two tracks, chosen in the menu; off by default). scripts/story_music.py builds both.

music_dayone.m4a (the default track)

Work:       Hans Zimmer, Day One (Interstellar, 2014)
Recording:  commercial soundtrack recording supplied by the site owner
Changes:    excerpted (10.75-110.75 s), crossfaded into a loop and loudness-levelled

music_loop.m4a (the second track) and the soundtrack of story_1080p.mp4 / story_720p.mp4

Work:       Ludwig van Beethoven, Symphony No. 7 in A major, Op. 92, II. Allegretto (public domain)
Recording:  orchestral recording supplied by the site owner
Changes:    excerpted, faded, looped and loudness-levelled
