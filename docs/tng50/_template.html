Mock quasar sightlines through the circumgalactic medium of one TNG50-1 galaxy (subhalo 488530 at z = 0), with optical depths from Trident. Choose a sightline, then change the resolving power R and the S/N per resolution element: every panel is recomputed in your browser. A new geometry can be cast on demand when a sightline server is online.

How to read this

Rows are three far-UV transitions: O VI λ1032 (warm-hot gas), H I Lyβ and C III λ977 (cooler gas). Columns are four spectrograph setups: two resolving powers crossed with two S/N values.

Higher R narrows the line-spread function and splits blended velocity components. Lower S/N buries weak components in the noise. The intrinsic overlay shows the spectrum before any instrument.

Galaxy: TNG50-1 subhalo 488530, a central Milky-Way-mass disc galaxy at z = 0. Sightlines are labelled by orientation (face-on, edge-on or random), impact parameter b and azimuth Φ from the projected major axis.

Noise is drawn in the browser, so it matches the publication figure only statistically; the model curves match the Python pipeline to 10−6. “Publication figure” renders the matplotlib version on the server.

Simulation: IllustrisTNG, TNG50-1 (Nelson et al. 2019; Pillepich et al. 2019), used under the IllustrisTNG data policy. Spectra: Trident (Hummels, Smith & Silvia 2017) on yt (Turk et al. 2011). Instrument model: Gaussian LSF with FWHM = λ0/R, white noise per pixel, 5 pixels per resolution element. Source code: github.com/tanmay-starslab/cgm-absorber-requirements.
