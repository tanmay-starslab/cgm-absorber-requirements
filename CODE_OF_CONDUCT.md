# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and assume good
faith. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or any other
project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the maintainer privately through the contact form at
https://tanmaysingh.uk/#contact. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject
contributions that do not follow this code.
